# 4. TYPES OF INFORMATION COLLECTED AND THE PURPOSES

The type of information that you may provide Canon Canada or that Canon Canada may collect is tied to the purposes for which the information is collected. Often, it is tied to the products or services you request, or to the nature of the interaction between you and Canon Canada. Examples of this are set out below.
